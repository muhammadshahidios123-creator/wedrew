User Data Deletion

Clear instructions for revoking application access and requesting removal of data processed by PageFlow Agent.

On this pageRequest deletionRemove access on FacebookWhat deletion includesWhat happens nextContact

1. Submit a data deletion request

You may ask us to delete Facebook-related personal data processed or stored by PageFlow Agent. To submit a request:

  1. Send an email to inamullahbaryar@gmail.com with the subject PageFlow Agent — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the Facebook Page name or Page ID associated with the app and a contact email so we can locate your records. Do not send your Facebook password or access token.
  3. We may ask for reasonable verification to ensure the requester is authorized before removing associated records.

Email deletion request →

2. Remove access on Facebook

You can also revoke the integration from your Facebook account’s settings under Business Integrations (or the equivalent app connection settings currently shown by Meta). Find PageFlow Agent and remove its permissions. Revoking access prevents future authorized API actions; it does not necessarily delete copies of records previously stored by this app, so email us if deletion is needed.

3. Data covered by the request

Depending on what has actually been stored, deletion may include Facebook user and Page identifiers, access tokens, saved upload metadata, queued content, uploaded temporary files, and operational records linked to your account. Data retained only where legally necessary is handled according to applicable law. Content already published to Facebook may need to be removed separately from the Page through Facebook tools.

4. After we receive your request

We will review the request, verify authorization if needed, and process eligible deletion without undue delay. We will reply to your email with the outcome or any additional details needed to complete the request. This page provides instructions, not an automatic deletion callback API.

5. Need assistance?

For questions about deletion, access revocation, or privacy, email inamullahbaryar@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
