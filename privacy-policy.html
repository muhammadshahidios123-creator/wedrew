Privacy Policy

How PageFlow Agent processes information associated with authorized Facebook Page publishing.

On this pageOverviewData we processWhy we use dataSharing & storageSecurity & retentionChoices & deletionPolicy updatesContact

1. Scope and overview

Effective date: October 15, 2026. This policy explains how PageFlow Agent (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when an authorized user connects a Facebook Page or uses our video publishing workflow. This policy covers the app itself, not merely this informational website.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, we may process:

  • Facebook account identifiers or basic profile details exposed by approved authorization.
  • Page identifiers, Page names, Page access tokens, and relevant Page management permissions.
  • Videos supplied for publishing, captions, titles, descriptions, scheduling choices, and upload status.
  • Operational records such as timestamps, publishing results, and technical error logs needed to provide or troubleshoot the service.
  • Communications you send to our support email.

We do not ask you to share your Facebook password with us.

3. How we use information

We process permitted information to identify Pages you are authorized to manage, authenticate authorized API actions, publish content you request, operate or troubleshoot an authorized workflow, address support requests, maintain app security, and fulfill deletion obligations. We do not use Facebook data for unrelated advertising or sell it.

4. Sharing and storage

We may transmit the content and related Page information needed to fulfill an authorized publishing action to Meta through its APIs. If third-party infrastructure providers process data on our behalf, they must be used only to support the service and subject to appropriate security and contractual controls. We do not publish your content to a Page without an authorized workflow.

Exact hosting providers, storage locations, and data subprocessors must be confirmed and reflected here before any real user data is processed.

5. Security and retention

Access tokens should be stored securely, never included in publicly accessible code, and available only to authorized services. We aim to retain data only as long as needed to deliver the service, resolve issues, or meet lawful requirements. Final retention periods must reflect the actual backend implementation.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Facebook access through your Facebook settings. You can also request deletion of personal information processed by PageFlow Agent by emailing inamullahbaryar@gmail.com, or following the steps on our Data Deletion page. We will review and process legitimate requests without undue delay, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in features, processing activities, legal obligations, or Meta requirements. The effective date will be updated when revisions are published.

8. Contact

For questions about privacy, permissions, or personal data: inamullahbaryar@gmail.com.

Before app submission: Verify that each data category, storage practice, retention statement, and contact detail matches the real app and backend. Publishing this template does not itself guarantee Meta approval.
